Privacy Policy

Quraan Arabi Academy ("the Academy," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data and privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, submit a recitation or placement evaluation, apply for a programme, or purchase a course or digital product, as well as how we use, store, protect, and process that data. By using our website or enrolling in any programme, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. What Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Academy, we may collect:

  • Identity Data: Full name, age, and optional profile photo.
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and country of residence.
  • Academic & Evaluation Data: Current reading or memorisation level, submitted recitation audio recordings, diagnostic notes, study goals, and learning progress.
  • Transaction Data: Programme or plan selected, billing status, payment references, and transaction records (excluding full card numbers).
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and general website interaction data.
Recitation & Placement Audio: Audio recordings submitted for placement or recitation evaluation are used solely by the Academy and authorised instructors for educational assessment and personalised guidance. They are stored securely and are never sold or shared with third parties for commercial advertising.
2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Reviewing placement or recitation evaluations and processing programme applications.
  • Scheduling and delivering live online sessions via our Academy Classroom, Zoom, Google Meet, or approved web conferencing tools.
  • Sharing academic feedback, session reminders, and progress updates.
  • Managing student enrolment, billing records, and support requests.
  • Improving curriculum structure, learning tools, and website stability.
  • Sending educational updates or programme information where permitted or consented to.
  • Complying with applicable legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

We never sell your personal data to third parties and do not use student academic or audio data for third-party ad profiling.

3. Data Storage, Security & Local/Cloud Architecture

We apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards (including encrypted connections in transit and access controls limited to authorised personnel) to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

Current & Cloud Storage: Depending on the active feature of our platform, certain learning preferences may be stored locally in your browser (localStorage) while core enrolment, progress, and evaluation records are stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase / PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security and strict access controls).

Hub-and-Spoke Communication Privacy: Personal phone numbers and private contact details are not shared publicly on the platform or exposed unnecessarily between parties; official coordination is handled through designated Academy channels.

Security Notice: No internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% immune to risk; however, we take prompt action to address any security incident. Please keep your login links, access codes, and device credentials confidential.
4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses essential technical storage/cookies required for core page functionality, language direction, and session state. Any analytics tools used are configured to understand site performance in aggregate without sharing sensitive evaluation recordings with ad networks. You may manage browser storage or cookie settings at any time.

Similar rights may apply under applicable state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA in California.

By submitting information from outside Egypt, you acknowledge that your data may be processed or stored on servers outside your home country subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Children's & Youth Data (Minors)
  • Under 16: Applications and accounts for learners under 16 must be submitted and managed by a parent or legal guardian.
  • Ages 16–17: Learners aged 16 or 17 may participate with verified parental or guardian consent.

1. Scope of Services

Quraan Arabi Academy provides online educational services for non-Arabic speakers and international learners, including:

  • Structured Arabic reading, recitation, and Tajweed learning paths.
  • Intensive / high-commitment mentorship programmes (such as The Quraanic Transformation).
  • Selective scholarly mentorship tracks (such as the Ijazah Track).
  • Digital educational materials (such as PDF guides, practice sheets, or study kits) where made available.

All sessions are delivered online. Learners are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection, working audio/video equipment, and access to the agreed meeting platform. The Academy is not responsible for interruptions caused by local device, speaker/microphone, or internet issues on the learner's side.

2. Eligibility & Selective Admission

To use our services or enrol in a programme:

  • You must provide accurate and truthful information during evaluation and registration.
  • Learners are welcomed starting from 2 years of age; learners under 18 years old require parent or guardian consent and involvement.

Admission to selective or intensive mentorship programmes (including The Quraanic Transformation and the Ijazah Track) is subject to evaluation and review. Submitting an application or evaluation does not automatically guarantee acceptance, and the Academy reserves the right to decline an application fairly where suitability or schedule alignment is not met.

3. Pricing, Currency & Payment Processing

Programme fees, payment schedules, and applicable currencies are communicated clearly prior to payment confirmation.

  • Currency Conversion & Gateway Rules: Where fees are quoted in USD (for example, 500 USD for an intensive programme) and processed through a local, regional, or international payment gateway (such as Paymob, Kashier, Payoneer, PayPal, or Stripe), currency conversion, card issuer rules, or bank fees may apply depending on the learner's card and bank.
  • Secure Processing: Payments are processed through authorised third-party payment gateways; the Academy does not store raw credit or debit card numbers.
  • Taxes & Bank Charges: Any taxes, duties, or bank charges imposed by the learner's bank or jurisdiction are the responsibility of the learner unless explicitly stated otherwise.
4. Scheduling, Rescheduling & Attendance

Session schedules are coordinated according to the agreed plan and time zone.

  • Standard Rescheduling Notice: Rescheduling requests for regular Academy tracks must be submitted at least 6 hours before a scheduled session (and at least 24 hours for Ijazah Track sessions, as specified in the Ijazah Track Special Agreement).
  • Late Apology Grace (< 6 Hours): If a learner apologises less than 6 hours before a regular session, they receive one (1) grace allowance; after that, any late cancellation is counted as a used session.
  • Unannounced Absence (No-Show): Each learner receives one (1) unannounced no-show per month without deduction; any further no-show without prior notice is counted as used.
  • Monthly Reschedule Cap & Validity: A maximum of 2 reschedules per month is permitted. Remaining sessions in a package stay valid for 1 month and are rarely carried over to the following month except in exceptional circumstances approved by Administration.
  • Late Arrivals: If a learner joins late, the session ends at its scheduled time so subsequent students are not delayed.
  • Instructor Unavailability: If a session cannot be held due to an instructor or Academy issue, it will be rescheduled or credited at no extra cost.
5. Student Conduct & Academic Integrity

Learners must engage respectfully, honestly, and in good faith. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Misrepresenting identity, age, reading/memorisation level, or using another person or AI/pre-recorded audio to complete assessments.
  • Recording live sessions or private feedback without prior explicit permission.
  • Copying, distributing, reselling, or publicly posting Academy lessons, workbooks, audio, or proprietary materials without written permission.
  • Abusive, disrespectful, or unlawful behaviour toward instructors, staff, or other learners.

Violation of these rules may result in suspension or termination of access in accordance with the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Enrolment grants a personal, non-transferable licence for individual study only.

Qur'anic reading, Tajweed mastery, and scholarly certification depend on genuine effort, consistency, practice, and mastery — not merely payment or elapsed time. While we provide structured instruction and personal guidance, specific academic outcomes or timelines cannot be guaranteed as an automatic commercial result.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Academy is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from use of the website or enrolment. Nothing in these Terms limits mandatory consumer rights that cannot be waived under applicable law (including EU / UK consumer protection rules where applicable).

8. Subscription Cancellation & Account Termination

Recurring subscriptions (where applicable) can be cancelled prior to the next billing cycle so future renewals do not occur, taking effect at the end of the current paid billing period unless a refund window applies under the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Ijazah Track Special Agreement

Issued under: Quraan Arabi Academy · Founder's Office (Mohamed Abdul-Maqsoud). This Special Agreement governs participation in the Ijazah Track at Quraan Arabi Academy, a selective scholarly mentorship conducted under the direct supervision of Mentor Mohamed Abdul-Maqsoud. By accepting admission and proceeding with the Ijazah Track, you agree to be bound by this Agreement alongside the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

This is a binding scholarly agreement. Acceptance of a place in the Ijazah Track constitutes your explicit acceptance of all terms in this document, in addition to the General Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund & Cancellation Policy. Please read carefully before proceeding.
1. Selective Admission & Personal Nature
  • Admission to the Ijazah Track is selective and is not automatic. Each applicant is evaluated individually for recitation/reading readiness, sincerity, discipline, and suitability.
  • The Academy and Mentor reserve the right to accept or decline any application at their discretion without obligation to state a reason.
  • Admission is personal and non-transferable; another person may not attend or take your place.
2. Mastery Over Fixed Timelines
  • The Ijazah Track is measured by mastery, accuracy, and scholarly readiness, not by a fixed calendar deadline.
  • Any time estimates shared during onboarding are indicative only and do not guarantee completion within a specific number of weeks or months.
Legitimate scholarly transmission is earned through precision and consistency, never purchased by elapsed time.
3. Attendance & Preparation

Consistent attendance and preparation are required:

  • Learners must give at least 24 hours' advance notice when requesting to reschedule an Ijazah Track session.
  • Repeated unexcused absences or consecutive missed sessions without prior approved notice may lead to a formal warning, suspension, or removal from the track.
  • Learners must complete assigned review, recitation, and preparation before each session.
4. Scholarly Conduct & Respect

Because Ijazah involves scholarly transmission and personal mentorship, learners must maintain adab (proper etiquette), humility, sincerity, and respect toward the Mentor and Academy staff at all times.

Disrespectful, abusive, or dishonest conduct, or any action that harms the integrity of the mentorship or the Academy's standing, may result in immediate termination without refund.

5. Academic Integrity
  • Learners must present their own genuine recitation and work at all times.
  • Impersonation, using another reciter's audio, or relying on AI/unauthorised tools during evaluation or recitation is strictly prohibited and results in immediate dismissal and forfeiture of fees/privileges.
  • No learner may claim or announce an Ijazah from Quraan Arabi Academy unless and until formal issuance is completed by the Founder.
6. Certification & Ijazah Issuance
  • Completing reading or Tajweed stages grants stage/course certificates where applicable, whereas Ijazah with Sanad is a distinct scholarly trust granted solely upon the Founder's personal academic judgement that the required mastery, accuracy, and adab have been fulfilled.
  • Payment of fees, attendance, or time spent does not by itself guarantee the granting of an Ijazah.
  • The Founder retains the right to withhold certification if standards are not met, or to revoke certification if material misrepresentation, academic dishonesty, or serious misconduct is later established, with written notice.
The Ijazah is a Sacred Scholarly Trust: It carries the weight of an unbroken chain (Sanad) connecting the holder to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Its issuance is strictly a scholarly decision — never a commercial transaction.
7. Confidentiality

Private mentorship feedback, session recordings (where permitted), evaluation notes, and proprietary training materials are strictly confidential and may not be recorded without permission, shared with third parties, or published without prior written consent.
